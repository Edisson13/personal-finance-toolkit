3. Where to find these numbers? (10-Q / 10-K Cheat Sheet) SEC Filings & Ratio Formulas
Click to Toggle Guide

Every public company files quarterly (Form 10-Q) and annual (Form 10-K) reports with the SEC (Item 8: Consolidated Financial Statements). Below is a visual map of the three core financial statements showing the exact raw line items to locate and how to turn them into the screener ratios above.

1. Consolidated Income Statement
Profitability & Growth

Measures operational performance over a period (Quarter or Trailing Twelve Months - TTM).

Revenue (Net Sales) Top-line total sales
Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) Direct production cost
= Gross Profit Revenue − COGS
Operating Income (EBIT) Gross Profit − OpEx (R&D, SG&A)
Net Income (Bottom Line) Profit after Interest & Taxes
Diluted EPS (Earnings Per Share) Net Income ÷ Diluted Shares
2. Consolidated Balance Sheet
Solvency & Net Worth

A snapshot at a single point in time of what the company owns (Assets) vs. owes (Liabilities).

Cash & Short-Term Investments Liquid war chest
Total Current Assets Cash + Receivables + Inventory (<1 yr)
Total Current Liabilities Payables + Short-Term Debt (<1 yr)
Total Debt (Short + Long-Term) All interest-bearing obligations
Total Shareholder Equity (Book Value) Total Assets − Total Liabilities
3. Statement of Cash Flows
Real Cash Generation

Tracks actual cash entering and leaving the bank account across Operating, Investing, and Financing activities.

Operating Cash Flow (OCF) Cash generated by core business
Capital Expenditures (CapEx) Cash spent on Property, Plant & Equip.
= Free Cash Flow (FCF) Operating Cash Flow − CapEx
Common Dividends Paid Cash returned to shareholders

How to Calculate the Input Metrics from Raw 10-Q / 10-K Line Items

P/E Ratio & Earnings Yield
P/E = Share Price ÷ Diluted EPS

Or Market Cap ÷ Net Income. Earnings Yield % is the inverse: (EPS ÷ Price) × 100.

P/B Ratio (Price-to-Book)
P/B = Market Cap ÷ Shareholder Equity

Compares market valuation to net accounting assets on the Balance Sheet.

Debt / Equity & Current Ratio
D/E = Total Debt ÷ Shareholder Equity

Current Ratio = Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities (tests 12-month liquidity).

PEG Ratio (Lynch Formula)
PEG = P/E Ratio ÷ Fwd EPS Growth %

A stock at 20x P/E growing EPS at 25%/yr has a PEG of 20 ÷ 25 = 0.80.

Gross & Operating Margin %
Gross % = (Rev − COGS) ÷ Rev × 100

Operating Margin % = (Operating Income ÷ Revenue) × 100. Indicates pricing power.

ROE % (Return on Equity)
ROE = (Net Income ÷ Equity) × 100

Measures how efficiently shareholder capital is compounded into bottom-line profit.

ROIC % (Return on Invested Cap)
ROIC = NOPAT ÷ (Debt + Equity − Cash)

Where NOPAT = Operating Income × (1 − Tax Rate). Prevents debt-driven ROE distortion.

FCF Yield %, P/FCF & Payout %
FCF Yield = (OCF − CapEx) ÷ Market Cap

P/FCF = 100 ÷ FCF Yield %. Payout Ratio = Dividends Paid ÷ Net Income.
