Personal Finance & Net Worth Suite

A private, zero-dependency financial ecosystem that runs 100% locally in your browser. Model multi-asset investing, eliminate debt, factor in cost of living & inflation, and project your total net worth into the future.

📌 Important: Planning Engine vs. Historical Progress Tracker
The calculators here (Investing, Debt Payoff, Cost of Living, and the Net Worth Command Center) are forward-looking planning tools used to model growth, simulate compound interest, and project scenarios. To record your actual real-world monthly progress over time and compare reality against your plan, use the dedicated Monthly Net Worth Log & Progress Tracker module. Files created in the Tracker module should be stored and loaded exclusively within the Tracker module.

Quick-Start Guide: How to Use This Suite

Four simple steps to model, connect, and back up your complete financial life offline

Step 1 Model Your Modules

Open any calculator below—such as Multi-Asset Investing, Debt Payoff, or Cost of Living—and enter your current balances, interest rates, and monthly cash flows.

Tip: All charts & tables update in real time
Step 2 Export Module CSVs

Click the green Export CSV button in the top-right corner of any calculator to save your exact configuration and year-by-year projection to your computer.

Tip: Files open in Excel or reload back into any tool
Step 3 Combine for Net Worth

In the Net Worth Command Center right below, load your exported Investing, Debt, and Cost of Living CSVs (or type numbers directly) to see where you stand today and project your future wealth.

Tip: Automatically links debt payoff rollovers
Step 4 Track Reality vs. Plan

Use the Net Worth Command Center to plan scenarios. Once per month, log your real balances in the Monthly Tracker to build a permanent timeline and see if you are ahead of or behind your plan!

Tip: Tracker CSVs are dedicated to the Tracker module
